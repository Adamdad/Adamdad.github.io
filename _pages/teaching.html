Teaching

A growing collection of interactive, in-browser demos that visualize neural-network and sequence-modeling concepts — from a single neuron's forward pass to recurrent networks and multi-head attention. Drag the sliders, step through, and watch the math move.

These widgets accompany DSAI4207 lectures on neural-network foundations and self-attention. Each card opens a standalone demo page — click Open Demo to explore it.
