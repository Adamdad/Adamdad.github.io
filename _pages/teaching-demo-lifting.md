A line separates at most `3/4` XOR corners in 2D. Press **Lift** to add `x₁x₂`, then drag the 3D view: a plane now separates `4/4`, while the 2D boundary becomes a cross.

**What to look for:** one new coordinate makes the points separable by a flat boundary.
