    01From scores to probabilities

    The model speaks in scores, not probabilities.

    Each word gets a raw score, a logit. Softmax turns any scores into probabilities that add up to 100%. Drag a handle.

    ✎ Pause & predict

    Add +1 to every score. What happens to the probabilities?

    Nothing changes. Softmax only sees score differences — try “Shift all +1”. Multiplying the scores does reshape it: that is what temperature does.

    Only score differences matter. Softmax = exponentiate, then normalise.

    02Greedy decoding

    Always pick the favorite.

    The simplest decoder: read the context, get probabilities, take the tallest bar, append it, repeat — until <eos>.

    ✎ Pause & predict

    Greedy took the best word at every step. Is the finished sentence the most likely sentence?

    Not necessarily. Greedy looks only one word ahead. A locally great word can lead into a poor continuation — and it can never go back. Next: keep a few options open.

    03Beam search

    Keep a few options open.

    Keep the B best partial sentences. Each round: extend every one, rank by total probability, prune the rest.

    05Modify the logits

    Edit the scores before the choice.

    Greedy can get stuck: same context, same favorite, forever. A logit processor sits between the model and softmax and rewrites the scores. First rule: every word loses λ points for each time it has already been used. Raise λ.

    ✎ Pause & predict

    A penalty breaks the loop. Is a bigger penalty always better?

    No. “the” and “.” have to come back in normal text, and the penalty punishes them too. Push λ past 1 and the article drops out: “and knight sleeps”.

    This is an additive frequency penalty. Libraries ship variants: Hugging Face’s repetition_penalty divides or multiplies the score instead, and no_repeat_ngram_size bans a repeat outright. Click any word to see how it was chosen.

    Takeaway

    A logit processor rewrites the scores before softmax — here z′ = z − λ × count.
